A Compact Sofa With Full-Size Comfort

Crate & Barrel · Petrie 76" Midcentury Apartment Sofa

Product

Why We Like It

Most apartment sofas save space by shrinking the seat, which is the part you sit on. Petrie keeps a 23-inch seat depth and a 19-inch seat height, which is a proper full-size sit, and saves the space in the arms instead: slim 3.5-inch track arms on a 76-inch frame.

It is a genuine 1960s silhouette, with boxy hand-tufted cushions and tapered ebony legs, and a Crate & Barrel classic with hundreds of reviews behind it. The menswear-style heathered fabric hides wear and reads as tailored rather than plush.

The 34-inch diagonal depth is the number that matters for older buildings: this sofa fits through doors and up stairs that stop deeper sofas.

Design Notes

  • The upright sit suits reading and conversation; it is not a nap sofa.
  • Legs show the floor beneath, which makes a small room feel larger; keep the rug low-pile so the line stays clean.
  • Works well with a single lounge chair angled toward it rather than a matching loveseat.

What to Consider

Firm and upright by design. If you want to sink in, this is the wrong sofa; look at the Lounge apartment sofa in the same family.

Button tufting collects crumbs in a house with small children.

Why It Is in Editor's Choice

Full seat depth in a 76-inch frame + slim arms + fits through narrow doors + a proven design

Styling Pairing

Pair with:

  • A round wood or marble coffee table
  • A brass or black floor lamp
  • One lounge chair in a contrasting wood frame
  • A 6-by-9 or 8-by-10 flatweave rug
