A Bed That Grows With the Room

Crate & Kids (Crate & Barrel) · Bodie Natural Oak Wood Spindle Kids Storage Bed

Product

Why We Like It

The mistake with kids' beds is buying something childish that has to be replaced at ten. Bodie avoids it: a spindle headboard with a gentle curve, slightly angled back legs, and a natural oak finish. It is a classic bed scaled for a child's room, and it will read as a grown-up bed when the room grows up.

The two drawers in the base are the practical reason to choose it over a plain frame. They are on metal glides, can be mounted on either side, and take the toys and bedding that otherwise live on the floor.

GREENGUARD Gold certification matters more in a child's bedroom than anywhere else in the house.

Design Notes

  • The tall headboard (40 inches) gives enough back support for reading together; allow for it against a window sill.
  • Choose the drawer side before delivery based on which side is open in the room.
  • Natural oak pairs with almost any wall color; keep bedding simple and let the wood do the work.

What to Consider

Oak veneer over engineered wood on some panels means it should be treated like fine furniture, not scrubbed. It is durable, not indestructible.

The full size is generous for a small room; the twin version has the same design in a smaller footprint.

Why It Is in Editor's Choice

Classic proportions that age well + built-in storage + safety and air-quality certifications + solid construction

Styling Pairing

Pair with:

  • A simple wood nightstand from the same collection or a small stool
  • A washable low-pile rug
  • A wall-mounted reading light rather than a table lamp
  • White or natural linen bedding with one accent color
