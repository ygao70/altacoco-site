An Outdoor Sofa That Does Not Look Like Outdoor Furniture

Crate & Barrel · Mallorca 85" Wood Outdoor Sofa with Ivory Cushions

Product

Why We Like It

Mallorca reads like a low, wide Scandinavian sofa that happens to live outside. A plinth base, wide flat arms, and a linear eucalyptus frame in a grey driftwood finish, with deep ivory cushions on top. Put it under a covered patio next to a low table and it is an outdoor living room.

The cushion construction is what separates it from furniture that looks similar and fails in two summers: acrylic covers that shed water and resist fade, foam cores wrapped in polyester, and mesh panels underneath so the cushion dries from the inside.

Eucalyptus is a dense hardwood that handles weather well for its price; wire-brushing it means small scuffs disappear into the texture.

Design Notes

  • A 35-inch seat depth is lounging depth; pair it with a low table no higher than the 12-inch cushion top.
  • In the Pacific Northwest, stand cushions on edge after rain and store or cover them over winter.
  • The wood will silver further with sun; a yearly wipe-down with wood cleaner keeps it even.
  • Ivory shows dirt; the Sunbrella colorways cost more and hide more.

What to Consider

At 85 inches wide and 37 deep it is a full-size sofa; measure the patio and the path to it.

Eucalyptus is not teak. It needs slightly more care and will not last as many decades.

Why It Is in Editor's Choice

Indoor proportions outdoors + real weatherproof cushion construction + wire-brushed hardwood + modular family

Styling Pairing

Pair with:

  • A concrete or teak low table
  • Two matching lounge chairs from the same collection
  • An outdoor rug in a flat weave
  • Large planters at both ends to frame it
