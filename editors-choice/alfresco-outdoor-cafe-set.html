A Dining Set Sized for Balconies

Crate & Barrel · Alfresco 35" Black Metal Outdoor Cafe Table Set with Silver Sunbrella Cushions

Product

Why We Like It

A 35-inch square is the largest table that still works on a typical apartment balcony, and Alfresco makes that footprint feel like a real dining spot rather than a bistro afterthought. Black slatted aluminum, clean lines, and low-profile Sunbrella cushions that stay out when it drizzles.

The materials are the practical case. Aluminum does not rust; the powdercoat holds up in sun; the faux-wood slats give the warmth of wood without the annual oiling. It is the set you can leave outside.

With over six hundred reviews it is one of Crate & Barrel's most-owned outdoor pieces, which counts for something in a category full of new designs.

Design Notes

  • On a balcony under 6 feet deep, use two chairs and keep the other two inside for guests.
  • The umbrella opening means a small market umbrella fits without a separate base on a sunny balcony.
  • Black metal gets hot in full afternoon sun; the cushions are the point of the set.

What to Consider

Faux-wood slats are a good imitation, not real wood; if you want the real thing, the Mallorca collection is the step up.

Do not leave glassware on the tabletop in strong sun; the slats can mark under magnified heat.

Why It Is in Editor's Choice

Balcony-scale footprint + rustproof, leave-it-out materials + Sunbrella cushions + a long track record

Styling Pairing

Pair with:

  • A small planter or two at the rail
  • A string of warm outdoor lights overhead
  • A washable outdoor runner under the table
  • A compact side table for the drinks
