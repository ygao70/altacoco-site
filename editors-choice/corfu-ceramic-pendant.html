Editor's Choice  /  Lighting

A Pendant That Can Carry the Room

Crate & Barrel · Corfu Large Cream Ceramic Pendant Light 18"

Product

Why We Like It

Most statement pendants shout. Corfu does the opposite: a single tapered ceramic shade in matte cream, 18 inches across, with a soft curve and a slightly rustic surface. It reads as an object rather than a fixture, which is what makes it able to anchor a room without dating it.

Ceramic is the right material for this job. It has weight and texture that painted metal does not, and it throws a warm, downward pool of light that suits a dining table.

At under $400 it is inexpensive for a fixture that does this much work, and the brass hardware ties it to the lamps and pulls most people already own.

Design Notes

  • Sized for a table 42 to 60 inches wide; over a longer table, use two or step up to a linear fixture.
  • Hang the bottom of the shade 30 to 36 inches above the tabletop.
  • Use a warm (2700K) LED and a dimmer; the fixture is dimmer compatible.
  • Suitable for slanted ceilings; not for bathrooms.

What to Consider

The shade is opaque, so light goes down, not out. In a dining room lit only by this pendant, add a lamp or sconce elsewhere for ambient fill.

Ceramic is heavy for its size; confirm the junction box is rated for the fixture weight and have it professionally installed.

Why It Is in Editor's Choice

Sculptural without being trendy + warm ceramic light + brass details + strong value

Styling Pairing

Pair with:

  • A wood dining table in oak or walnut
  • Linen or paper table lamps elsewhere in the room, so the finishes relate
  • A rattan or woven element nearby to echo the handmade texture
