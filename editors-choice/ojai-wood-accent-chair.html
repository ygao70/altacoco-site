A Well-Proportioned Lounge Chair Under $1,000

Crate & Barrel · Ojai Wood Accent Chair

Product

Why We Like It

Ojai borrows from Shaker and Danish Modern at once: an exposed oak frame with proper mortise-and-tenon joints, softly rounded edges, shaped armrests, and tight seat and back cushions angled for a relaxed sit. It is the kind of chair that looks right in a warm-minimal room and still looks right after the sofa changes.

The dimensions are the reason it earns the value label. At 33 inches wide and 36 deep it has real lounge-chair presence, but the open wood frame keeps it visually light, so it works in a room where a fully upholstered chair would feel heavy.

The $999 price is for the stocked fabric; the chair is a Crate & Barrel exclusive with a solid review history.

Design Notes

  • Reads best in pairs flanking a fireplace or window, or as a single contrast chair beside a linen sofa.
  • The 16.5-inch seat height is on the low, relaxed side; pair with a side table around 20 inches.
  • The exposed frame benefits from a rug underneath so the legs do not look stranded on a bare floor.

What to Consider

Tight (non-removable) cushions mean spills are spot-cleaned, not laundered; choose a mid-tone fabric for a family room.

This is a lounge chair, not a deep armchair. If you want to curl up sideways, look at something wider and softer.

Why It Is in Editor's Choice

Danish-Shaker proportions + visible joinery + open, light footprint + under $1,000

Styling Pairing

Pair with:

  • A small round oak side table
  • A linen or wool throw over the arm
  • A floor lamp with a linen shade behind one shoulder
  • A second Ojai for a conversation pair
