Upholstery Options for Homes With Kids

Fabric, leather, slipcovers, and the details around them that matter more than the material itself.

Article

Product Comparison · 4 min read

Every sofa in a house with children becomes a fort, a snack table, and occasionally a trampoline. The question is not how to prevent that but how to choose a piece that looks fine afterward.

Performance fabric

The default answer, and usually the right one. Look for a W or W/S cleaning code, a fiber with inherent rather than sprayed-on stain resistance, and a rub count above 30,000. Mid-tone colors and subtle texture hide far more than a flat pale weave. Our guide to what actually matters in performance fabric covers the details.

Leather

Leather wipes clean, does not hold crumbs, and improves with age in a way fabric never does. The catch is which leather. Top-grain and full-grain with a protected (pigmented) finish are the family-proof kind; aniline leathers are gorgeous and absorb every spill; bonded leather is not really leather and peels within a few years. Leather scratches from pets and zippers, so it suits some households better than others.

Slipcovers

A washable slipcover is the only upholstery that resets fully. Modern slipcovered sofas fit far better than the baggy versions of twenty years ago, and a second cover in a different color is a cheap way to change the room. The trade-offs are a softer, less tailored look and the effort of removing and replacing a cover after washing.

The details that matter more

  • Removable cushion covers. If seat and back cushion covers zip off, most accidents become laundry rather than professional cleaning.
  • Reversible cushions. Doubles the life of the seat.
  • Tight-back vs. loose cushions. Tight backs have fewer crevices for crumbs; loose cushions are more comfortable and can be flipped.
  • Frame and legs. A kiln-dried hardwood frame and a low, solid base survive climbing. Tall thin legs and light frames do not.
  • Rounded edges. On coffee tables especially, but on sofa arms too.

A reasonable combination

For most families: a performance fabric in a mid-tone, removable and reversible cushion covers, a low solid base, and a rug that is easier to clean than the sofa. Leather if pets are not scratchers. Slipcovers if you want to stop worrying entirely.
